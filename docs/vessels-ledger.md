# /vessels claims ledger

Every checkable claim on `/vessels`, with how it was checked. **Sourced** entries were read in the named source while writing (November 2026 session). **Computed** entries are calculated by `src/vessels-models.js` at runtime and covered by `tests/vessels-models.test.js`. **Derived** entries follow arithmetically from sourced values. Illustrative choices are labeled on the page.
