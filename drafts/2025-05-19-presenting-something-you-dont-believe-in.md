I don't love presenting in front of people, but there's one thing (weird trick)
that makes night and day difference in my ability to present something well:
whether I believe in it or not, or whether I'm proud of the work.

I've worked at jobs in leadership positions where I had to present, just for the
sake of presenting. Because that's what you do. When I've managed to have
something my team did that was Good Work, it naturally presents itself. When
that's not true, it hurts the soul.

When I observe others' presentations, it seems to be the latter kind most of the
time. Managers who've forgotten what it feels like to actually building
something, and have become mere politicians and beaurocrats, regurgitating
nonsense purely posturing and trying to make others believe they have any idea
they know what they're doing. Delete your account.
